# Home — Ferretería Pino

Scope: index.html (single page). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: neighbours and trades in a Spanish barrio; job: know what's stocked, where the shop is, whether it's open now, how to call/WhatsApp; many need a key copied today.
Action: visit the shop (Cómo llegar / open-now status) or WhatsApp. Proof: since 1985, key-cutting specialty, Cadena88 member. Stock photos (Pexels) stand in for real ones.
Constraints: no backend, no e-commerce, Spain example data (€, +34), Spanish copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the key counter of the shop — the blank-key wall with numbered hooks and the cutting machine. It refuses the category default (tool hero photo + red banner + row of category cards) and the old dark neon/glass look.

OWN-WORLD: Cool nickel-white ground (#ecedea), graphite ink (#1b1c1e), brushed brass (#b4873a) as the one material colour carried by keys, tags and the primary action; a small signal red only for the "abierto" pulse and Cadena88. Flat fills, no gradients, no glass, no glows. Hairline steel rules, punched tags with a round hole, numbered hooks (Nº 01…), key bitting profile as the divider line. Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display, monumental) + Archivo (text, labels in expanded caps, tabular numbers).

STORY: Visitor sees the name and a key being cut, learns it's open now, where it is and that copies are done on the spot; scrolls the hook wall of key types, the hanging catalogue tags by category, services, the 1985 story, and ends at the visit panel (hours with today highlighted, map, WhatsApp, form).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top hook-rail nav (logo tag left, numbered links, WhatsApp right). Left-aligned headline "Ferretería Pino" at ~15vw-scale Bricolage across the top half; under it one line of offer and live status chip "Abierto · cierra 20:30" + address, primary brass "Cómo llegar" and secondary "WhatsApp". Bottom: one giant SVG key spanning the full width, bow at left stamped PINO·1985, blade running off the right edge. Signature interaction: on load a cutter wheel travels the blade and carves the bitting 1-9-8-5, brass shavings drop; hover/scroll re-trace.

FORM: "El panel de llaves" — my top-ranked grounded candidate (#1 of 7), chosen by the user as the pick card; seed key 3a9e0dab. Raises kept: monumental single-grotesk type as material; flat colour discipline (no blends).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Memorable moment: the key cut 1·9·8·5 in the hero; key blanks swinging on their hooks.
